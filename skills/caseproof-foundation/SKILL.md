# caseproof-foundation

## Authority
Follow `AGENTS.md`, `CONSTITUTION.md`, `frontend/BLUEPRINT.md`, accepted ADRs and generated backend contracts before this skill. Do not invent backend capabilities or legal approvals.

## Procedure
Treat original DOCX, inventory and exhibits as immutable evidence. Backend generated contracts and authorization own all verification states. Distinguish identity, page mapping, fidelity, coverage and human semantic review. A source match never proves the legal proposition. Never silently turn OCR ambiguity into support. Respect matter-level isolation, no confidential analytics, NL/FR/EN, accessibility and signed human decision receipts.

## Acceptance
State exact files changed, tests actually executed, evidence and open gates. Stop when required source authority, permission or data provenance is absent. See [task checklist](references/TASK.md).
