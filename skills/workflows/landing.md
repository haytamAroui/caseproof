# CaseProof landing-page workflow

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `frontend/BLUEPRINT.md`, current product status and approved claims.
2. Build a clear lawyer-facing narrative around a **labelled synthetic** exhibit-reference near-miss. Show a source crop and why a seemingly plausible reference requires review.
3. Explain actual capabilities, supported deployment modes and limitations without unsupported privacy, bar, accuracy or court-certification promises.
4. Provide visible demo controls for pause/replay/manual inspection. Reduced-motion must preserve meaning and controls.
5. Keep legal terminology reviewed in EN/FR/NL, with native practitioner review outstanding until actually completed.
6. Test keyboard, screen reader semantics, responsive layout, contrast, performance and links/forms. Do not claim screenshot or browser QA unless run.
7. Keep marketing assets and scripts separate from the authenticated evidence workspace. Never transmit confidential documents to analytics or third parties.

Relevant skills when installed: `caseproof-foundation`, `caseproof-landing-page`, `caseproof-claims-copy`, `caseproof-motion`, `caseproof-animation-review`, `caseproof-diagram-flow`, `caseproof-i18n-accessibility`, `caseproof-landing-qa`.
