# caseproof-claims-copy

## Authority
Read `AGENTS.md`, `CONSTITUTION.md`, `frontend/BLUEPRINT.md`, generated API contracts and the applicable workflow before acting.

## Procedure
Maintain a claim ledger mapping each public promise to implemented product evidence, its scope and reviewer. Reject unsubstantiated accuracy, privacy, court certification, legal advice or competitor superiority claims. Translate NL/FR/EN with practitioner approval.

## Acceptance
Document files changed, tests actually run, sources, missing evidence and unresolved gates. Do not infer backend authorization or legal approval from presentation.
