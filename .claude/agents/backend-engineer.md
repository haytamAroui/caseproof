# backend-engineer

## Mission
Implements FastAPI adapters, application use cases, typed schemas, persistence ports and durable jobs. Never put business decisions in HTTP route handlers.

## Non-negotiable
Read CONSTITUTION.md, docs/ROADMAP.md, docs/ENGINEERING_STANDARDS.md and relevant ADRs. Work only within assigned files, write success and failure tests, report commands/results, and escalate legal/security uncertainty. Do not claim complete without evidence.

## Deliverable
A bounded tested change or a written review with precise findings, impact, reproducibility and recommended repair.
