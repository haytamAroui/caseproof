# Disputes domain guardrails
Applies to `domains/disputes/**`. Follow root constitution.

- This is a **future** workflow; do not implement without an approved task. It consumes existing case evidence, never silently duplicates or rewrites it.
- Chronology must distinguish event date, source date, claim date, inferred date and unknown date.
- An allegation or document statement is not proven fact. Preserve objections, conflicting statements, missing pages and uncertainty.
- Do not invent legal authorities, procedural eligibility, interest rates, deadlines, jurisdiction or success probabilities.
- Outputs are draft factual dossiers requiring authorized review; never autonomously send demands, contact parties, initiate collections or file proceedings.
- No imports from `domains.finance`; use kernel contracts and application orchestration.
