# Draft Data Processing Agreement — CaseProof pilot

**WORKING DRAFT — NOT EXECUTED; legal review required.** Parties: [Belgian law firm, address, registration] (Controller) and [CaseProof legal entity, address, registration] (Processor). Effective date: [date]. Main services agreement: [reference].

## 1. Instructions and scope
Processor processes personal data only on documented Controller instructions to operate the CaseProof exhibit-reference review pilot. No model training, advertising, secondary analytics or transfer to another controller is authorized. Processor immediately informs Controller if an instruction appears to violate applicable data-protection law, unless prohibited by law.

## 2. Annex A — processing details
Purpose: upload, parse, index, review and export litigation draft and exhibits. Categories of subjects: clients, opposing parties, witnesses, staff and persons named in dossiers. Data: identity, case facts, legal claims, correspondence and potentially special-category or criminal-offence data. Nature: storage, OCR, indexing, retrieval, reviewer decisions and deletion. Duration: pilot term plus agreed retention period, subject to documented deletion instructions and applicable obligations. **No real client data until annexes are completed.**

## 3. Confidentiality and security
Personnel authorized to process data are bound by confidentiality. Processor implements documented technical and organizational measures: least privilege, private matter-scoped access, encryption in transit and at rest where independently evidenced, logging, patching, backup and incident procedures. Legal professional secrecy obligations are additional to GDPR obligations and require Belgian counsel approval.

## 4. Subprocessors and transfers
Controller grants [specific/general] written authorization for subprocessors listed in Annex B (legal name, purpose, location, safeguards, contact, contract). Notify Controller of proposed changes with [X] days' notice and opportunity to object. Flow down equivalent obligations. **EU-only hosting is a proposed requirement, not an assertion of verified residency.** No international transfer without documented Chapter V safeguards and Controller authorization.

## 5. Assistance and rights
Processor assists Controller with data subject requests, security, breach assessments, DPIAs and consultations, considering nature of processing and available information. Controller remains responsible for lawful basis, transparency and legal obligations.

## 6. Breaches
Notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach, with known nature, affected data, impact, mitigation and updates. Define operational escalation channel and target notice window in Annex C.

## 7. Deletion and return
At Controller choice, return or securely delete personal data on termination or documented request, except where retention is required by law. Include originals, OCR sidecars, derived text, review records, temporary files and logs as appropriate. Backup expiry and any legally required residual retention must be documented and auditable. A proposed 30-day pilot expiry is **not implemented or guaranteed**.
