# ADR 0002-kernel-extraction

**Status:** accepted as initial design baseline, subject to empirical and legal validation.

## Decision
CaseProof is an independent repository. Reuse AZComply source normalization, quote verification, evidence hashing and provenance algorithms through a domain-neutral API, with regression tests and license review. Avoid shared runtime, immediate forced package extraction, EU AI Act SystemFacts or classification rules.

## Revisit criteria
A demonstrated business, correctness, reliability, privacy or cost requirement that the chosen architecture cannot satisfy.
