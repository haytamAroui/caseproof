# ADR 0003-automation-boundary

**Status:** accepted as initial design baseline, subject to empirical and legal validation.

## Decision
Automated extraction and source matching are permitted; legal entitlement, IOS eligibility and externally consequential actions remain independently gated. Source match != factual truth != legal conclusion. Exception review uses explicit materiality rules, not universal 95% scores.

## Revisit criteria
A demonstrated business, correctness, reliability, privacy or cost requirement that the chosen architecture cannot satisfy.
